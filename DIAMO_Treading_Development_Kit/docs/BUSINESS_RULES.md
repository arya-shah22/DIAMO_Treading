# Business rules
## Arithmetic
Use decimal.js; store integer paise and hundredths of carat. Round monetary line totals half-up to paise; deterministic largest-remainder allocation for expenses and cost residuals. Rate and percentage inputs may have higher internal precision. Validate finite nonnegative quantities/rates, positive transaction quantities, less 0–100%, nonnegative invoice totals. Dates local calendar ISO YYYY-MM-DD; due date = invoice date + nonnegative due days (zero same day).
## Purchase
Gross = carat × INR rate; net = gross × (1−less/100). Allocate each expense across lines proportional to net values; if total net zero and expenses positive reject pending explicit allocation rule. Supplier payable = net + supplier-billed expenses; separately paid expenses credit chosen cash/bank. Inventory cost = net + all expenses. One lot per purchase line; no GST. Example net 190000 and 294000, expense 5000 => 1962.81 and 3037.19 allocated. Debit inventory, credit supplier and cash/bank respectively.
## Sale
Gross and less per line as purchase. Brokerage fixed INR or percentage of after-less amount, per line OR invoice (not double counted), broker required when positive. Invoice adjustment signed addition/deduction; customer invoice = sum net + adjustments; brokerage never reduces customer receivable. Net proceeds = invoice − brokerage; trading profit = proceeds − COGS. Debit customer/credit sales (signed adjustment reflected in revenue); debit COGS/credit inventory; debit brokerage expense/credit broker. Brokerage payment debits broker, credits funds; never expense twice. Example gross 100000, less 5%, adjustment +1000, brokerage 2% = 1900, COGS 80000 => customer 96000, profit 14100.
## Inventory
Opening + purchases − sales per category. FIFO by acquisition date then stable lot ID; only lots available as of sale date. Manual override selects quantities from eligible same-category lots, total exactly sale quantity. No negative stock at any historical point. Preserve allocations and residual cost; final depletion consumes all residual paise. Example 50ct @4000 +100ct @5000, sell 40 FIFO => COGS 160000, remaining 110ct cost 540000. Manual from second => COGS 200000. Backdated edits must validate downstream movements; preserve valid manual allocations, block unsafe edits rather than silently change historical cost. Hike = category override if not null else company default; apply to each remaining lot cost; projected value = cost × (1+hike/100); never post projection to ledger. Category average = remaining cost/remaining ct.
## Payments
Manual allocation or oldest invoice date then ID; support multiple bills and partial payments. Same company/party/direction only; cannot allocate more than outstanding or payment. Excess/unallocated = advance. Supplier advance asset/customer advance liability; applying advance clears invoice and advance without moving cash again. Broker payment settles brokerage payable. Cancel/edit must reverse allocations and journal; block if dependent advances already used unless safely reversed. Cash/bank negative allowed only with warning confirmation.
## Loans
Simple interest, exact elapsed days (exclude start day); monthly P×R/100×days/30, yearly /365 even leap year. No compounding. Return date optional, interest continues to actual settlement. Interest-only payments allowed; principal unchanged until full repayment. Accrual reporting default: recognize earned/incurred interest through report date without double counting posted accruals; settlement clears accrued interest. Reject interest over accrued unless business rule approved. Loan taken debit funds credit liability; given debit asset credit funds; reverse principal at closure. Separate interest income/expense and receivable/payable. Opening loans preserve original start and prior settled/accrued interest.
## Accounting
Double entry, accrual basis proposed default. Every posted journal balanced; account types asset/liability/equity/income/expense. Opening balances balanced, no unexplained plug. Capital/drawings via JV. Transfers debit destination credit source; no income/expense. JV cannot directly change inventory, COGS, loan principal or invoice control balances without corresponding subledger workflow; party adjustments require explicit reconciliation. Reports use effective dates and active postings; reversal/repost audit retained. Prior FY profit rolls into equity for balance sheet without resetting balances; P&L period only. Number sequences per company/type/FY: PUR, SAL, PAY, REC, LT, LG, JV, TRF /2026-27/0001; new FY resets sequence, no reuse; retain ID on edits, block FY-changing edits or cancel/re-enter.
## Defaults requiring implementation review
Separate DB per company; daily backup + exit, retain 30; oldest-first payment; first-login password change recommended; invoice adjustments treated as revenue adjustments, not actual third-party costs (record actual costs via JV); accrual basis and rounding conventions proposed previously. For complex historical edits, block and explain dependencies rather than silently recost. No tax compliance claims.
